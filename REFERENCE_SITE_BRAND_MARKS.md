## Visible mark bounds

The imported sources retain their original transparent canvas. Measured visible bounds show that Aston Martin, Audi, and Bentley are wide-and-shallow (`900×270`, `860×315`, and `900×328` within `1024×1024`), while Rolls-Royce is tall (`534×872` within `1024×1024`). The interface therefore uses identity-specific containment limits rather than a single square scaling rule.
